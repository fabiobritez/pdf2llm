## Summary
### Key features
- Bidirectional, low-power, zero-drift, voltage-output current-sense amplifier with a -0.2 V to +40 V common-mode voltage range [p1].
- Single 1.7 V to 5.5 V supply; 50 µA at 25°C (typ) quiescent current and 100 nA (max) shutdown current [p1].
- Low 500 pA (typ) input bias current, ±15 µV (max) offset voltage, and 80 nV/°C (max) offset drift [p1].
- High accuracy: 132 dB (min) CMRR, 7 ppm/°C (max) gain error drift, and ±0.2% (max, A1) to ±0.4% (max, A5) gain error [p5].
- Fixed gain options: 25 V/V (INA190A1), 50 V/V (INA190A2), 100 V/V (INA190A3), 200 V/V (INA190A4), and 500 V/V (INA190A5) [p1].
- Voltage output and reference (REF) pins; ENABLE is available only in SOT-23 (DDF) and UQFN (RSW) packages. UQFN, SC70, and SOT-23 packages are specified from -40°C to +125°C [p1, p3].

### General operation
- A capacitively coupled input amplifier feeds a difference amplifier output stage, producing V_OUT = (I_LOAD × R_SENSE × GAIN) + V_REF [p14].
- Supports both high-side and low-side current sensing across the -0.2 V to +40 V common-mode range, independent of V_S [p15].
- Grounding REF configures unidirectional sensing; applying an external voltage to REF offsets the output for bidirectional sensing [p16].
- Pulling the active-high ENABLE pin to ground enters shutdown (10 nA (typ) quiescent current) and sets OUT to high impedance for ADC multiplexing [p19].
- Senses voltage across an external shunt resistor to drive an ADC for overcurrent protection, precision current measurement, and closed-loop feedback [p1].
