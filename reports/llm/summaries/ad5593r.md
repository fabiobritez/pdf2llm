## Summary
### Key features
- 8-channel, 12-bit configurable ADC/DAC/GPIO with integrated 2.5 V, 20 ppm/°C reference and temperature indicator [p1].
- Supply: V_DD 2.7 V min to 5.5 V max, V_LOGIC 1.8 V min to V_DD max; power-down I_DD 3.5 µA max [p5].
- ADC: 12 Bits typ resolution, 2 μs max conversion time, 500 ns min track time, -2 LSB min to +2 LSB max INL [p3].
- DAC: 12 Bits typ resolution; INL and DNL each -1 LSB min to +1 LSB max, with outputs unloaded and linearity calculated over codes 8 to 4085 [p3, p6].
- DAC AC and reference: 6 μs typ settling time for a ¼-to-¾-scale step, 1.25 V/μs typ slew rate; V_REF output 2.495 V min, 2.5 V typ, 2.505 V max [p4, p17].
- 2-wire I^2C interface (100 kHz standard mode, 400 kHz fast mode) [p20].
- 16-lead TSSOP, 16-lead LFCSP, and 16-ball WLCSP packages; -40°C to +105°C temperature range [p1].
### General operation
- Eight I/O pins configure via I^2C as DAC outputs, ADC inputs, or GPIOs for control and monitoring [p1].
- Multiplexer and sequencer route selected channels (0 V to V_REF or 0 V to 2 × V_REF) to the ADC [p18].
- Resistor-string DACs with rail-to-rail buffers span 0 V to V_REF or 0 V to 2 × V_REF, updating via LDAC [p17].
- Pins also support push/pull or open-drain GPIO, three-state, and default 85 k Ω pull-down modes, plus power-down control [p26].
- Control pins: SCL, open-drain SDA, A0 (7-bit slave address LSB), V_REF (1 V to V_DD external), and active-low RESET [p8].
