## Summary
### Key features
- Synchronous buck-boost converter: adjustable TPS63070, fixed 5 V TPS630701, and adjustable TPS630702 with 200 Ω typ output discharge [p1, p3, p7].
- VIN is 2.0 to 16 V once started with VOUT ≥ 3.0 V; startup with VOUT < 3.0 V requires VIN ≥ 3.0 V. TPS63070/TPS630702 have a 2.5 to 9 V output range; TPS630701 has a fixed 5 V output [p6, p7].
- Up to 95% efficiency; 2 A output current in buck mode and in boost mode at VIN = 4 V, VOUT = 5 V. Average positive input current limit is 3600 mA typ (3050 mA min, 4150 mA max) at VIN = 5.0 V, VOUT = 6.5 V, Tj = 0 to 125 °C [p1, p7].
- 50 µA typ quiescent current [p1]; +/- 1% dc accuracy in PWM mode and +3% / -1% in PFM mode with VIN ≥ 3 V [p7].
- 2400 kHz typ (2100 kHz min, 2700 kHz max) oscillator, synchronizable from 2100 kHz min to 2800 kHz max [p7].
- 2.5 mm x 3 mm nom VQFN package; -40 °C min to 125 °C max operating junction temperature [p1, p4].

### General operation
- Four internal N-channel MOSFETs with average current mode control and internal compensation automatically transition between synchronous buck and boost operation [p9, p10, p17].
- PS/SYNC selects forced PWM (low), Power Save Mode (high, PFM below approximately 650 mA inductor current), or external clock synchronization [p3, p14].
- Adjustable versions regulate FB to 800 mV typ; VSEL/FB2 scale VOUT, precise EN (0.8 V typ rising) sequences startup, and open-drain PG indicates regulation [p3, p6, p7].
- Integrates soft start, load disconnect, UVLO, overvoltage, and 160 °C typ thermal shutdown for dual Li-Ion, metering, camera, portable PC, and medical applications [p1, p6, p12].
