## Summary
### Key features
- Dual 5V, 2A synchronous step-down DC/DC converter with 19mΩ NMOS and 75mΩ PMOS switches, 0.5V to V_IN V_OUT range, and ±1% V_OUT accuracy [p1].
- Operating supply voltage (V_IN) is 2.25V min to 5.5V max; regulated feedback voltage (V_FB) is 495mV min, 500mV typ, 505mV max [p3].
- Oscillator frequency (f_SW) is 1.85MHz min, 2MHz typ, 2.15MHz max (1MHz min to 3MHz max sync); minimum on time at V_IN = 5.5V is 25ns typ, 45ns max [p4].
- V_IN quiescent current is 1.2μA typ, 2μA max in shutdown and 45μA typ, 70μA max with only one buck enabled, in regulation and sleeping in Burst Mode (T_A = 25°C, V_IN = 3.3V) [p3].
- Available in 12-lead 2mm × 2mm × 0.74mm LQFN and 16-ball 1.64mm × 1.64mm × 0.5mm WLCSP packages [p1]. Automotive-qualified versions are the listed LQFN #W variants [p3].
- Operating junction temperature ranges are -40°C to 125°C, -40°C to 150°C, and -55°C to 150°C [p2].
### General operation
- Two internally compensated, peak current mode buck regulators switch 180° out of phase, comparing FB pins to an internal 500mV reference [p12].
- The MODE/SYNC pin selects pulse skipping mode (low), forced continuous PWM mode (floating), or Burst Mode (high) [p12].
- A 1MHz to 3MHz clock on MODE/SYNC synchronizes the internal PLL; PGOOD flags outputs below 97% or above 110% after 120μs [p13].
- Precision 400mV threshold EN1/EN2 pins control shutdown and sequencing, with soft-start, current limiting, and short-circuit protection [p12].
- Typical applications include distributed DC power systems (POL), FPGA/ASIC/μP core supplies, servers, telecom supplies, optical networking, and industrial/automotive/communications [p1].
