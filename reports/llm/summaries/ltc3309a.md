## Summary
### Key features
- Monolithic synchronous 6A step-down DC/DC converter with Silent Switcher architecture, pin compatible with LTC3307(3A) and LTC3308(4A) [p1].
- Input voltage (V_IN) of 2.25V min to 5.5V max; V_OUT from 0.5V to V_IN with ±1% accuracy over temperature [p1, p3].
- Internal switches have 31mΩ typ PMOS and 8mΩ typ NMOS ON-resistance at V_IN = 3.3V and T_A = 25°C; minimum on-time is 22ns typ (42ns max) at V_IN = 5.5V over the specified junction temperature range; maximum duty cycle is 100% min [p3].
- Frequency programmable or synchronizable from 1MHz min to 3MHz max, with a 2MHz typ (1.9MHz min, 2.1MHz max) default [p3].
- At T_A = 25°C and V_IN = 3.3V, quiescent current is 40μA typ (60μA max) sleeping in Burst Mode and 1.2mA typ (2mA max) in all modes while not sleeping, excluding switching currents; shutdown current is 1μA typ (2μA max) at V_EN = 0.1V [p3, p4].
- Available in a 12-lead 2mm × 2mm × 0.74mm LQFN package with -40°C to +125°C, -40°C to +150°C, and -55°C to +150°C operating junction temperature grades. AEC-Q100 automotive versions are available as the designated #W products listed on p2 [p1, p2].

### General operation
- Constant-frequency peak current mode control with internal compensation compares FB to a 0.5V typ (0.495V min, 0.505V max) reference [p3, p11].
- MODE/SYNC selects pulse skip (low), forced continuous (floating), or Burst Mode (high), or synchronizes the PLL to an external clock [p9, p11, p12].
- RT sets the 2MHz typ default (at V_IN) or resistor-programmed frequency; includes a 400mV EN threshold, open-drain PGOOD, soft-start, and current/overvoltage/thermal protection [p3, p9, p11, p12].
- Used in optical networking, servers, telecom, automotive, industrial, distributed DC power (POL), FPGA/ASIC/μP core supplies, and battery-operated systems [p1].
