## Summary
### Key features
- 4-channel PMBus Power System Manager that sequences, trims, margins, and supervises four supplies while monitoring input current and energy. [p1]
- Operates from V_PWR (4.5V min to 15V max, V_DD33 floating) or from V_DD33 tied to V_PWR (3.13V min to 3.47V max), drawing 10mA typ (13mA max). [p5]
- Output voltage ADC (0V min to 6V max) provides 122µV/LSB typ resolution and ±0.25% of Reading max Total Unadjusted Error (≥1V). [p6]
- Output current sensing spans -170mV to 170mV differential, with ±0.3% of Reading max error for sense magnitudes of 20 to 170mV and ±60µV max for magnitudes ≤20mV; 10 Bits typ DACs update every 250µs typ in Fast Servo Mode. [p6]
- Input current sensing achieves ±0.6% of Reading max Total Unadjusted Error (100mV, High Range) and 85dB min DC CMRR. [p8]
- Supports 10kHz min to 400kHz max PMBus clock. EEPROM endurance is 10,000 cycles min with writes at 0°C < T_J < 85°C; retention is 20 years min at T_J < 105°C for EEPROM cycled fewer than 10,000 times. [p9–p10]
- Packaged in 64-Lead (9mm × 9mm) Plastic QFN and 63-Pin (7.5mm × 6.25mm × 1.52mm) BGA for 0°C to 70°C or -40°C to 105°C. [p4]
### General operation
- Internal 16-bit ADC monitors four output voltages, currents, and external temperatures, input voltage/current, and die temperature, calculating power and energy. [p1]
- Controls DC/DC converters via trim pins or feedback networks using closed-loop servo trimming, margining, and time-based, tracking, or cascade sequencing. [p19]
- Supervises input voltage and channel voltages, currents, and temperatures; fault responses include continuing, latching off, or sequencing off with retries. [p19]
- Coordinates multi-device operation via SHARE_CLK, FAULTB0/FAULTB1, CONTROL, ALERTB, and power-good pins, includes a watchdog timer, and logs faults to EEPROM. [p19]
