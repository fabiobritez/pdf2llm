## Summary
### Key features
- Monolithic synchronous step-down Silent Switcher DC/DC converter delivering up to 15A, with V_OUT from 0.5V to V_IN and ±1% remote-sense accuracy [p1].
- Operating V_IN range is 2.25V to 5.5V. At T_A = 25°C and V_IN = 3.3V, quiescent current is 1.3mA typ (2.0mA max), excluding switching currents; shutdown current is 1μA typ (2μA max) with V_EN = 0.1V [p3–p4].
- Internal switches have 8mΩ typ top and 3mΩ typ bottom ON-resistance at T_A = 25°C and V_IN = 3.3V; the top switch current limit is 18A min, 21A typ, 24A max for V_OUT/V_IN ≤ 0.2 and current flowing out of SW [p3].
- R_T-programmed switching frequency ranges from 0.5MHz to 5MHz; with RT tied to V_IN, the default frequency is 2MHz typ (1.8MHz min, 2.2MHz max). Minimum on-time is 35ns typ (60ns max), and maximum duty cycle is 100% min [p3].
- Precision 0.4V typ EN rising threshold, PGOOD output, SSTT soft-start/tracking, 4mV/°C typ temperature monitor, and 0.5MHz to 2.25MHz external synchronization with RT tied to V_IN [p3, p16].
- 18-pin (3mm × 3mm × 0.95mm) LQFN package with exposed pad PGND, rated -40°C to 125°C (LTC3313E) and -40°C to 150°C (LTC3313J) [p2].
### General operation
- Constant-frequency peak current mode control: an error amplifier compares FB to a 500mV reference to servo ITH, with ±100mV remote AGND sense [p11].
- Operates in forced continuous or pulse-skipping mode (2MHz default clock), or synchronizes via internal PLL to an external MODE/SYNC clock [p16].
- SSTT uses a 10µA pull-up for soft-start, tracks external inputs from 0V to 0.5V, and monitors die junction temperature after start-up [p18].
- Supports multiphasing parallel converters and industrial, communications, server, telecom, POL, and FPGA/ASIC/μP core applications. AEC-Q100-qualified automotive versions use the #W suffix; only the listed automotive-grade products are available for automotive applications [p1–p2].
