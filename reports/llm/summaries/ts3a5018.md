## Summary
### Key features
- Quad single-pole double-throw (SPDT) analog switch (TS3A5018) passing bidirectional signals from 0 V to V_+ [p1, p17].
- Single-supply 1.8-V to 3.6-V operation [p1], with recommended V_+ from 1.65 V min to 3.6 V max [p4].
- ON-state resistance r_on of 7 Ω typ (10 Ω max) and match Δr_on of 0.3 Ω typ (0.8 Ω max) at 3 V, 25°C [p5].
- 300 MHz typ bandwidth, 0.21% typ THD, and 2 pC typ charge injection at 3.3 V, 25°C [p5, p6].
- Switching times t_ON of 3.5 ns typ (8 ns max) and t_OFF of 2 ns typ (6.5 ns max) at 3.3 V, 25°C [p8].
- Positive supply current I_+ of 2.5 µA typ (7 µA max) at 3.6 V, 25°C [p6].
- Packaged in 16-pin SOIC, SSOP, TSSOP, TVSOP, UQFN, and VQFN [p1], operating over -40 °C min to 85 °C max [p4].
### General operation
- Four bidirectional SPDT switches each connect a common pin (COM1–COM4) to a normally closed (NC1–NC4) or normally open (NO1–NO4) pin [p3, p17].
- Shared digital pins /EN (active-low enable) and IN (path selector) accept 0 V min to 3.6 V max [p3, p4].
- With /EN low, IN low connects COM to NC and IN high connects COM to NO; /EN high turns all switches OFF [p17].
- Break-before-make switching (18.4 ns typ t_BBM at 1.8 V, 25°C) prevents shorting NC and NO in sample-and-hold, audio/video, and communication circuits [p1, p9, p17].
