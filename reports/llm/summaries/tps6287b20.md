## Summary
### Key features
- TPS6287Bxx family of pin-to-pin compatible 10A, 15A, 20A, 25A, and 30A synchronous step-down DC/DC converters with differential remote sense [p1].
- Input voltage range is 2.7V to 6V; three selectable output voltage ranges span 0.4V to 1.675V [p1], subject to V_OUT ≤ min(1.675V, V_IN − 1.5V) [p6–p7]. Output voltage accuracy is ±0.8% with V_IN ≥ V_OUT + 1.6V and droop compensation disabled [p9].
- Internal 2.6mΩ and 1.5mΩ power MOSFETs, and selectable 1.5MHz, 2.25MHz, 2.5MHz, or 3MHz switching frequency via FSEL on TPS6287BxxJE2 [p1].
- Quiescent current is 2.1 mA typ (4.6 mA max) with EN high, I_OUT = 0 mA, device not switching, and MODE low. Shutdown current is 18 µA typ (450 µA max) with EN low and V_SW = 0V; the shutdown maximum is at T_J = 125°C. Typical values are at V_IN = 5V and T_J = 25°C [p7].
- I^2C-equipped variants support an interface up to 3.4MHz; optional spread spectrum clocking and stacked operation are supported [p1]. VA variants have no I^2C interface [p3].
- Packaged in a 24-pin RZV (WQFN-FCRLF) measuring 3.05mm × 4.05mm nominal [p1].
- Operating junction temperature range of -40 °C min to 125 °C max [p7].

### General operation
- Uses a fixed-frequency DCS-Control topology comprising an inner emulated current loop and an outer voltage-regulating loop [p16].
- Operates in forced-PWM mode (PWM-CCM) or power-save mode (PWM-CCM, PWM-DCM, PFM-DCM), selected via MODE/SYNC or the CONTROL1 register [p18].
- LA0 and HA0 variants select start-up voltage through VSET1 and VSET2 and support voltage adjustment, monitoring, and warnings over I^2C [p1, p3]. JE2 variants use pin 3 as FSET [p4]; VA variants lack I^2C and also use VSET3 and VSET4 for start-up voltage selection [p3, p5].
- Provides COMP for external RC loop compensation, open-drain PG for power-good signaling, and SYNCOUT for stacked clock daisy-chaining [p5].
- Supports point-of-load remote sensing and parallel stacked operation for FPGA, ASIC, digital core, optical network, and storage supplies [p1].
